Peptide Companies

Peptide companies fall into two groups: manufacturers that control synthesis and testing, and resellers that repackage someone else’s product. The four signs below tell them apart.

Four signs of a legitimate peptide company

They disclose their synthesis

A real peptide company knows where its compounds are made. Vague origin stories and stock photos of glassware are warning signs.

They name their laboratory

Legitimate companies name the independent lab that verifies their lots and publish batch-specific results.

They put money behind quality

A written guarantee, such as our 200% commitment, signals a company confident enough to be tested.

They are reachable

A working contact channel, physical address, and responsive support separate companies from fly-by-night shops.

Frequently asked questions

What should I look for in peptide companies?

Disclosed synthesis origin, named independent testing, batch-specific COAs, a written quality guarantee, and reachable support.

Are all peptide companies manufacturers?

No. Many are resellers with no control over quality. Ask any company who synthesizes their compounds and who tests them.

Where is NeoPep Research based?

NeoPep Research compounds are synthesized in U.S. facilities and verified by independent accredited laboratories before release.
