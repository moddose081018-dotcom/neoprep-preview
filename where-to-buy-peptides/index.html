The five checks before you order

1. Demand a batch-specific COA

A COA that never changes between lots is marketing. The content of the certificate must match your exact batch number, and you should have access to it before ordering.

2. Confirm independent testing

Self-graded purity is meaningless. Look for verification by an accredited third-party laboratory using HPLC for purity and LC-MS for identity.

3. Check where it is synthesized

US synthesis and domestic fulfillment remove customs risk and shorten delivery windows. A company based in the USA shows its facilities; vague origin claims are a red flag.

4. Look at shipping conditions

Peptides are temperature- and light-sensitive in lyophilized powder form. Insulated, phase-change packaging is the minimum acceptable standard.

5. Test the guarantee

A supplier confident in its product puts money behind it. Our 200% guarantee pays double if independent testing fails a sealed vial, in writing, with clear terms.

Red flags to walk away from

  • No batch numbers on product pages or COAs
  • Purity claims with no named laboratory
  • Human-use claims on research compounds
  • Prices far below every comparable supplier
  • No physical address or working contact channel
