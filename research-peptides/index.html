Frequently asked questions

What are research peptides?

Research peptides are short-chain amino acid compounds supplied for in-vitro laboratory study, from peptide hormone signaling to tissue repair. They are not intended for human or veterinary use.

How do I verify the quality of research peptides?

Request the batch-specific Certificate of Analysis, confirm testing was performed by an independent lab, check that the company lists a physical address, and verify that identity (LC-MS) and purity (HPLC) are each reported as separate results.

Do you sell blends or only single compounds?

Both. Our catalog includes single compounds such as BPC-157 and Retatrutide, and research blends such as Wolverine (BPC-157 / TB-500) and CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin. First-time customers can ask our support team for help choosing the right compound.

FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY
